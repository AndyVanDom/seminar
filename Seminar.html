ARHL and Dementia

Hypotheses

  • Sensory Deprivation: Poor hearing leads to high cognitive load
  • Social Isolation: Depression from poor social interactions, low auditory input
  • Mediating Pathology: External mechanism acting on auditory pathway and brain
  • Causal Relationship: ARHL causes Dementia

Project Overview

Hypothesis

Using clear hearing and cognitive phenotypes, we can better understand the genetic relationship between ARHL and dementia.
